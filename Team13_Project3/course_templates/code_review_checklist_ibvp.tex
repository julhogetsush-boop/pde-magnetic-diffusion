\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item[$\square$] Grid coordinates, $h$, periodic endpoint convention, and array shapes are explicit.
\item[$\square$] Initial values and every boundary condition are enforced at the correct time level; compatibility is discussed.
\item[$\square$] Spatial-operator signs and advection upwind direction agree with the PDE.
\item[$\square$] Multistep start-up and final-time handling retain the claimed order or disclose the exception.
\item[$\square$] Sparse matrices/factorisations are reused where appropriate; solver failures and tolerances are checked.
\item[$\square$] Exact/reference solution is evaluated at the same physical time and grid points.
\item[$\square$] Norm scaling is correct; mass, energy, or total variation uses the stated grid convention.
\item[$\square$] Space and time refinements are separated and observed orders are computed from actual refinement ratios.
\item[$\square$] Stability tests excite the dangerous mode and avoid overflow being mistaken for evidence.
\item[$\square$] One-step hand test, constant/known-mode test, and regression test pass.
\item[$\square$] Results are regenerated from configuration, not hard-coded; randomness is seeded.
\item[$\square$] AI-assisted code/claims have a named independent check in the Claim Audit.
\end{itemize}
